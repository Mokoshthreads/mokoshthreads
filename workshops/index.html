More Than a Collection

Discover the Stories Behind the Objects

Mokosh Threads workshops are designed for curious beginners, collectors, creatives, and anyone interested in experiencing cultural objects beyond a display shelf.

Each workshop combines storytelling, practical demonstrations, guided activities, and carefully selected materials that participants can use during the session.
